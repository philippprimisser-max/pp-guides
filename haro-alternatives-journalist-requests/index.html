HARO alternatives in 2026: where to find journalist requests (and get them automatically)

Cision shut down the original HARO in late 2024 after the "Connectively" rebrand; Featured.com acquired HARO and restarted its email editions in April 2025. Meanwhile, requests spread across several platforms and social media.

Platforms

  • HARO / Featured: daily email requests (free), Featured adds matching on paid plans.
  • Qwoted: the most-named alternative by PR people and freelancers on Reddit.
  • Source of Sources: by HARO's original founder, email digest.
  • SourceBottle, Help a B2B Writer (B2B/SaaS), PressPlugs and JournoLink (UK).

Social media

Journalists still post call-outs with #JournoRequest, #PRRequest and similar hashtags, on X, Bluesky and Mastodon. Search "Latest" daily.

Collect them automatically

The Journalist Request Monitor on Apify collects public journalist requests, pitch calls and podcast-guest requests from public sources, filters by keywords, removes duplicates and delivers only new ones as CSV/Excel plus a digest. Run it daily on a schedule.

How to answer well

  • Only reply if you genuinely fit; off-topic pitches get people banned from these lists.
  • Answer the exact question first, in 2–4 sentences, then credentials and a link.
  • Respect deadlines and write your own reply; journalists increasingly filter AI-written pitches.
