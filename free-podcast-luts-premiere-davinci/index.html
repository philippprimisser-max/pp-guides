Podcast & talking-head LUTs: how to install and use them in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and CapCut

A LUT (look-up table, usually a .cube file) maps every colour in your footage to a new colour. For podcasts and talking-head videos it is the fastest way to give every episode the same look. It only works well if you correct first and apply the look second.

Want to follow along? Grab my 2 free podcast LUTs (Clean Bright and Warm Golden, .cube, Rec.709). It's €0+ on Gumroad, so free is fine. They work in Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut and CapCut desktop, not in the CapCut phone app.

1. Correct before you apply a look

  1. White balance: neutralise the white/grey areas (a wall, a shirt) so skin is not orange or green.
  2. Exposure: faces should sit roughly in the upper-middle of the waveform; don't clip the forehead.
  3. Log footage: if you shot S-Log, C-Log, V-Log etc., apply the camera maker's conversion to Rec.709 first. A creative LUT designed for Rec.709 will look wrong on flat log footage.

2. Install the LUT

Premiere Pro

Lumetri Color panel → Creative → Look → Browse… and pick the .cube file. Use the Intensity slider (often 50–80 % looks most natural on skin). Put corrections in Basic Correction, not in the LUT.

DaVinci Resolve

Project Settings → Color Management → Open LUT Folder, copy the files in, click Update Lists. In the Color page, add a serial node after your correction node, right-click → LUT → choose the file. Lower the node's Key Output Gain to reduce strength.

CapCut (desktop)

Adjustment → LUT → Import LUT, then drag the adjustment over the clip and lower the intensity.

3. Check skin tones

Turn on the vectorscope: skin of every complexion should sit near the "skin tone line". If a LUT pushes faces off that line, lower intensity or add a small hue correction after it. Check on a phone too; most podcast clips are watched there.

4. Make it repeatable

Save the correction + LUT as a preset (Premiere: Lumetri preset; Resolve: Grab Still / PowerGrade). Same camera and lights = one click per episode.
