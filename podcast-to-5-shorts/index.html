How I cut a 60-minute podcast into 5 Shorts in under an hour (free + paid tools)

One long episode can feed a week of Shorts. The workflow below takes about an hour for a 60-minute episode and works with free tools; one optional paid tool can do the clip-picking for you.

1. Get a transcript with timestamps

Run Whisper locally (free) or use one of the options in podcast to text. You need timestamps, because every later step works from the text instead of scrubbing audio.

2. Pick the 5 best moments

Manually: skim the transcript for strong opinions, numbers, stories with a turn and concrete advice. Each clip should be 20–60 seconds and its first sentence should work as a hook without context. Details: how to find the best clips in a podcast; hook ideas: Hook library for podcast clips.

Automatically: OpusClip (Werbung/Affiliate-Link · Ad / affiliate link) finds candidate clips and reframes them to vertical in the browser. It is fast, but it is a paid subscription beyond the free tier and its picks do not always match what your audience cares about, so review every clip before posting.

Cut each moment with ffmpeg: ffmpeg -ss 00:12:03 -to 00:12:51 -i ep.mp4 -c:v libx264 -c:a aac clip1.mp4

3. Add word-by-word captions

Most Shorts are watched muted. Burn in word-by-word captions for free: word-by-word captions with Whisper + ffmpeg.

4. Export 9:16 and check

  • 1080×1920, face and captions inside the safe zone (keep the bottom ~20% free for the app UI).
  • Hook in the first 2 seconds, no dead air at the start.
  • Consistent look: podcast LUTs.
