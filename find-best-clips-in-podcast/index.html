How to find the best clips in a podcast episode (manually and with AI)

Good clips are self-contained: they make sense without the rest of the episode, start with tension and end on a payoff. Finding them by scrubbing audio is slow; working from a transcript is much faster.

The manual method (about 20 minutes per hour of audio)

  1. Get a transcript with timestamps (see podcast to text).
  2. Skim for: strong opinions, numbers, stories with a turn, "the mistake I made…", disagreements, concrete advice.
  3. Mark start/end so the clip is 20–60 seconds and the first sentence works as a hook without context.
  4. Write 2–3 hook titles for each, then pick the best 3–5 clips per episode.
  5. Cut losslessly: ffmpeg -ss 00:12:03 -to 00:12:51 -i ep.mp4 -c copy clip1.mp4 (re-encode if the cut is not on a keyframe).

After cutting

  • Add word-by-word captions and a consistent look/LUT.
