How to monitor Bluesky (and Mastodon) for mentions of your brand or keyword

Bluesky and Mastodon don't have Twitter-style notification for keywords out of the box, but both are open enough to monitor.

Bluesky by hand

  • Search supports operators: "exact phrase", from:handle, mentions:handle, lang:en, since:2026-10-01, domain:example.com.
  • Sort by Latest and check daily. Pin a search or use a custom feed (via feed builders) for a keyword.

Mastodon by hand

  • Follow a hashtag: it appears in your home timeline. This is the most reliable way, because full-text search only covers posts whose authors opted in to being searchable.
  • Each server sees a different slice of the fediverse; large servers (mastodon.social) and topic servers (e.g. fosstodon.org for tech) cover more.
