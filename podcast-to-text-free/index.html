Podcast to text: 4 free and cheap ways to transcribe a podcast episode

Also in: Español · Português · Deutsch

Before you transcribe anything, check whether a transcript already exists. Then pick the cheapest method that is accurate enough.

Short version: if you just need one episode as text and don't want to install anything, paste the RSS, Apple Podcasts or MP3 link into my Podcast Transcriber on Apify (Apify's free monthly credit covers a short test). If you'd rather do it yourself for free, method 2 below works fine, and my free Whisper cheat sheet has the settings.

1. Look for an existing transcript (free)

Many shows publish transcripts in their RSS feed using the Podcasting 2.0 <podcast:transcript> tag, or on their website. Apple Podcasts and Spotify display auto-generated transcripts in their apps for many episodes, but don't offer an export. See how to download podcast transcripts from RSS.

2. Whisper on your own computer (free, slower)

pip install -U openai-whisper
whisper episode.mp3 --model small --language en --output_format txt

small is a good balance on a laptop; medium/large are more accurate but much slower without a GPU. Convert first if the file is huge: ffmpeg -i ep.mp3 -ac 1 -ar 16000 ep.wav.

3. Hosted Whisper by the minute

Services running Whisper large-v3 (for example Groq's API) transcribe an hour in well under a minute for a few cents. You need an API key and a little scripting.

4. From a link, no setup

The Podcast Transcriber on Apify takes an RSS feed, Apple Podcasts link or direct MP3 URL and returns TXT, SRT, VTT and JSON with word timestamps. It runs with or without your own Groq key and is billed per audio minute; Apify's free plan credit is enough to try it on a few short episodes. Spotify links don't work, see below.

What about Spotify links?

My transcriber doesn't take Spotify links, and that's on purpose: Spotify doesn't let you download episodes, and its terms of use don't allow it either. So I won't point you to tools that pull the audio out anyway. The good news is that almost every podcast you find on Spotify also has a public RSS feed; Spotify just plays it. Look up the same show on Apple Podcasts or podcastindex.org and use that link or the RSS feed instead. Spotify-exclusive shows have no public feed, so this route doesn't work for them.

Accuracy tips

  • Set the language explicitly instead of auto-detect for non-English shows.
  • Expect errors on names, jargon and crosstalk; search the transcript for proper nouns before publishing.
  • Only transcribe content you have the rights to use for your purpose.

Which one should you pick?

  • One episode, no setup: Podcast Transcriber from a link.
  • A show that already publishes transcripts: download them instead, with the Podcast Transcript Fetcher (episodes without a transcript cost nothing).
  • Lots of audio, a decent computer and some patience: Whisper locally, free.
